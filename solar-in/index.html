Kerala Solar Facts

Why Solar Makes Sense Across All of Kerala

Kerala has some of India's highest electricity tariffs — KSEB's telescopic tariff system means heavy consumers pay ₹7–₹9/unit. Solar directly attacks these upper-slab charges.

Even Wayanad — Kerala's lowest-sun-hours district — receives 4.7 peak sun hours daily, which is significantly better than much of North India. All 14 districts have viable solar ROI.

With PM Surya Ghar subsidy of up to ₹78,000 and KSEB net metering available statewide, solar investment payback across Kerala averages just 3–4.5 years.

Lowest sun hours (Wayanad) 4.7 hrs/day
