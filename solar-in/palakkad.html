Why Palakkad

Palakkad — Kerala's Solar Powerhouse with the Highest Sun Hours

Palakkad is exceptional among Kerala districts for one reason: sunlight. The Palakkad Gap — the natural pass through the Western Ghats — means the district receives significantly less cloud cover than coastal areas. With 5.4 peak sun hours daily, Palakkad consistently outperforms all other Kerala districts for solar generation.

This translates directly to faster payback periods. A 3kW system in Palakkad generates more electricity than the same system installed in Wayanad or Idukki — and often recovers the investment in under 3 years. For large agricultural and commercial loads, the ROI is particularly compelling.

FGE Solar serves Palakkad with MNRE-empanelled installations, KSEB net metering documentation, and PM Surya Ghar subsidy assistance. Our teams cover the full district.

Palakkad FAQs

Questions About Solar in Palakkad

Palakkad consistently receives the highest solar radiation in Kerala, with up to 5.4–5.6 peak sun hours daily. The Palakkad Gap's geographical feature means less cloud cover compared to coastal districts. This makes it one of the highest-ROI solar locations in the entire state.
Palakkad's higher temperatures can slightly reduce panel efficiency at the cell level (panels lose about 0.4% efficiency per degree Celsius above 25°C). However, the much higher irradiance more than compensates for this. Net output is still significantly higher than most Kerala districts.
We serve the entire Palakkad district including Palakkad city, Ottapalam, Shornur, Alathur, Mannarkkad, Chittur, Pattambi, Kunnamkulam border areas, Malampuzha, and all surrounding taluks.
