How to Check Your PM Surya Ghar Application Status in Kerala

Use the official portals to see the current stage of a rooftop-solar application, identify what is pending, and reach the right support channel without relying on WhatsApp forwards or unofficial status pages.

By FGE SolarPublished July 22, 2026Updated July 22, 2026

Your portal dashboard is the source of truth. Status labels, approval steps, and timings can vary by application and may change when a government portal or KSEB process changes.

Before you start: keep the registered mobile number, email address, KSEB consumer number, and any application or acknowledgement reference available. Never share an OTP with an installer or a third party.

Where Kerala applicants should look first

For the local electricity-connection side of a rooftop-solar application, use KSEB's e-Kiran solar rooftop portal. It provides a “Track my application” path and is managed by KSEB's Soura PMU.

For national PM Surya Ghar information, use the official PM Surya Ghar portal. Log in only through the official domain that you used to apply; do not enter credentials into links sent in unsolicited messages.

How to track the application

  1. Open the portal used for your application. Start with e-Kiran for the Kerala rooftop-solar workflow and use the PM Surya Ghar account for the national-scheme side.
  2. Sign in with the registered details. Use the mobile number or email connected to the application. If you cannot access the account, use the official password/username recovery route rather than creating a second application.
  3. Open the application or dashboard view. Read the exact pending message, not just a colour or label. Take a screenshot with the date if you need support.
  4. Check whether an action is requested from you. Common requests can include verifying contact details, supplying documents, confirming feasibility, or completing a bank-detail step. Follow only instructions shown inside the official portal.
  5. Contact the owner of the pending step. If the dashboard identifies a KSEB action, use the KSEB channel. For a portal-access problem, use the official portal support route. An installer can help explain project documents, but cannot override a portal decision.

What the status normally represents

A rooftop-solar journey has multiple checkpoints: registration, feasibility or connection review, vendor/project work, installation and commissioning, meter or grid work, and the subsidy process. The wording on your own dashboard matters more than a generic list because portals can rename or reorder stages.

Do not assume that an installation date, a meter update, and a subsidy-credit date are the same event. If the portal shows a task as pending, complete that task or ask the responsible authority what document or action is required.

If the status has not moved

  • Confirm that the registered mobile number and email still work.
  • Check the portal for a document, correction, or acknowledgement request.
  • Keep your application reference, consumer number, screenshots, and dates together before contacting support.
  • For Kerala utility-side questions, use KSEB's official e-Kiran contact options or KSEB customer support—not an unverified social-media account.
  • For national rooftop-solar scheme or portal issues, use the official contact or grievance route shown on the government portal.

Official sources for this guide

This page deliberately avoids promising approval or credit timelines. Portal rules and case-specific requirements can change, so verify a current application directly with the authority.

  • KSEB e-Kiran Solar Rooftop Portal — application tracking and Kerala rooftop-solar information.
  • PM Surya Ghar official portal — national scheme information.
  • MNRE/NIC rooftop-solar grievance guidance — official support routing.
