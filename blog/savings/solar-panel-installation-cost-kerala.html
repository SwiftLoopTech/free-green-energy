Solar Panel Installation Cost in Kerala 2026: 1kW, 3kW, 5kW and 10kW Price Guide

By FGE Solar Editorial Team · July 2026 · Updated 8 Oct 2026 · 12 min read

The first question almost every Kerala homeowner asks is what rooftop solar actually costs. The honest answer is that it depends on more than capacity, and any company quoting a single flat price per kilowatt without seeing your roof is guessing.

That said, there are reliable ranges. This guide breaks down solar panel installation cost in Kerala for 2026 by system size, explains what the PM Surya Ghar subsidy brings the price down to, shows what actually drives the difference between a cheap quote and a good one, and works through realistic payback periods.

Key Takeaways

  • On grid solar system price in Kerala in 2026 runs roughly Rs. 60,000 to Rs. 85,000 for 1kW, and around Rs. 4,50,000 to Rs. 6,50,000 for 10kW.
  • The PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana pays Rs. 30,000 per kW for the first 2kW and Rs. 18,000 for the third kW, capped at Rs. 78,000 for systems of 3kW and above.
  • There is no separate Kerala state solar subsidy. The central scheme is the only active residential subsidy.
  • A 3kW system is the value sweet spot for most Kerala homes, since it captures the maximum subsidy.
  • Off grid and hybrid systems cost 35 to 60 percent more than on grid because of the battery bank, and off grid does not qualify for subsidy.
  • Typical payback for a subsidised residential on grid system in Kerala falls in the range of 3.5 to 5.5 years.

Solar System Price in Kerala 2026: Size by Size

These ranges are for complete on grid installations including panels, inverter, mounting structure, cabling, protection devices, net meter coordination and commissioning. They are indicative and vary by brand, roof type and site conditions.

1kW Solar Panel Price in Kerala

Approximately Rs. 60,000 to Rs. 85,000 before subsidy. Suitable for very small households with monthly consumption under 100 units. Generates around 120 to 135 units per month. Needs roughly 60 to 80 square feet of shadow free roof.

2kW Solar System Price in Kerala

Approximately Rs. 1,15,000 to Rs. 1,60,000 before subsidy. Suits homes consuming 150 to 250 units monthly. Generates around 240 to 270 units per month. Needs roughly 130 to 160 square feet.

3kW Solar System Price in Kerala

Approximately Rs. 1,70,000 to Rs. 2,30,000 before subsidy. This is the most commonly installed residential size in Kerala. Suits consumption of 250 to 400 units monthly. Generates around 360 to 400 units per month. Needs roughly 200 to 240 square feet.

5kW Solar Panel Price in Kerala

Approximately Rs. 2,70,000 to Rs. 3,60,000 before subsidy. Suits larger homes with air conditioning, consuming 400 to 650 units monthly. Generates around 600 to 670 units per month. Needs roughly 330 to 400 square feet.

10kW Solar System Price in Kerala

Approximately Rs. 4,50,000 to Rs. 6,50,000 before subsidy. Suits very large homes, small commercial premises, guest houses and clinics. Generates around 1,200 to 1,350 units per month. Needs roughly 650 to 800 square feet.

Did You Know?

Cost per kilowatt falls as system size increases. A 1kW system might work out to Rs. 75,000 per kW while a 10kW system can land closer to Rs. 52,000 per kW, because fixed costs such as design, net metering paperwork, travel, scaffolding and commissioning are spread across more capacity.

PM Surya Ghar Subsidy in Kerala: What You Actually Receive

The central subsidy structure is fixed and does not vary by state.

  • 1kW system: Rs. 30,000
  • 2kW system: Rs. 60,000
  • 3kW system and above: Rs. 78,000, which is the maximum

The subsidy is calculated at Rs. 30,000 per kW for the first two kilowatts and Rs. 18,000 for the third kilowatt. Beyond 3kW nothing further is added, so a 5kW and a 10kW system both receive the same Rs. 78,000.

Solar Panel Cost After Subsidy in Kerala

  • 1kW: roughly Rs. 30,000 to Rs. 55,000 net
  • 2kW: roughly Rs. 55,000 to Rs. 1,00,000 net
  • 3kW: roughly Rs. 92,000 to Rs. 1,52,000 net
  • 5kW: roughly Rs. 1,92,000 to Rs. 2,82,000 net
  • 10kW: roughly Rs. 3,72,000 to Rs. 5,72,000 net

Subsidy Eligibility Conditions

  • You must be a residential consumer with a valid KSEB domestic connection.
  • The property must be owned by the applicant and have adequate shadow free roof area.
  • The system must be grid connected with net metering. Off grid systems do not qualify.
  • Installation must be done by a vendor registered on the National Portal for Rooftop Solar.
  • Panels and inverters must be from approved lists as specified under the scheme.
  • The subsidy is credited directly to your bank account after installation, inspection and net meter commissioning.

Scheme rules and approved vendor lists are periodically revised, so confirm current conditions with your installer before applying.

What Makes One Quote Cheaper Than Another

Two quotes for the same 5kW system can differ by a lakh. The gap is almost never arbitrary, and knowing where it comes from protects you.

Panel Technology and Brand

TOPCon and mono PERC modules from established manufacturers with strong warranty backing cost more than entry level modules. Panel efficiency also determines how much capacity fits on a constrained Kerala roof.

Inverter Quality

A tier one string inverter with a good service network in Kerala costs considerably more than a budget unit. Since the inverter is the component most likely to fail, this is a poor place to economise.

Mounting Structure

Hot dip galvanised or aluminium structures cost more than painted mild steel but survive Kerala humidity and coastal salt far longer. Elevated structures, sloped tile roof adaptors and ground mounts all add cost.

Balance of System

Correctly rated DC cable, UV stable conduit, quality MC4 connectors, DC and AC surge protection, and proper earthing add several thousand rupees but prevent the failures that make cheap systems expensive later.

Roof Type and Access

A flat concrete roof is the simplest and cheapest. Mangalore tile roofs, sloped RCC, asbestos sheets and multi storey buildings all increase labour, scaffolding and structure cost.

Site Location

Coastal installations need anti corrosion treatment. Remote hill locations add transport and logistics cost. Long cable runs from array to inverter or from inverter to the meter board add material cost.

Service and Warranty Terms

Quotes that include a free maintenance period, on site warranty support and subsidy paperwork assistance carry that value in the price. Compare like for like rather than headline figures.

Did You Know?

Roughly 50 to 55 percent of a rooftop solar quote is panels, 15 to 20 percent is the inverter, 10 to 15 percent is structure and balance of system, and the remainder is installation labour, design, documentation and margin. If a quote is dramatically below market, something in that stack has been substituted.

Off Grid and Hybrid Solar Costs in Kerala

If you need backup during outages, expect a significant premium over on grid pricing.

  • 3kW hybrid system with battery: approximately Rs. 2,80,000 to Rs. 4,20,000
  • 5kW hybrid system with battery: approximately Rs. 4,20,000 to Rs. 6,20,000
  • 3kW off grid system: approximately Rs. 2,70,000 to Rs. 3,80,000
  • 5kW off grid system: approximately Rs. 4,20,000 to Rs. 6,00,000

Battery chemistry drives most of the variation. Lithium iron phosphate costs more upfront than tubular lead acid but lasts two to three times longer, which usually makes it cheaper over the life of the system.

Commercial Solar Installation Cost in Kerala

Commercial and industrial systems price differently. Per kilowatt cost drops further at scale, typically landing between Rs. 40,000 and Rs. 55,000 per kW for systems above 25kW. Commercial buyers also access benefits residential buyers cannot: input tax credit on GST paid, and accelerated depreciation on the capital asset. Together these can materially improve the effective cost, though the PM Surya Ghar residential subsidy does not apply.

Payback Period and Long Term Savings

Take a 3kW system in Kerala as a worked example.

Assume an installed cost of Rs. 2,00,000 with a subsidy of Rs. 78,000, giving a net outlay of Rs. 1,22,000. The system generates roughly 4,300 to 4,600 units per year. At an effective KSEB domestic tariff in the region of Rs. 6.5 to Rs. 8 per unit for households in the middle slabs, that translates to annual savings of approximately Rs. 28,000 to Rs. 36,000.

That gives a payback period of roughly 3.5 to 4.5 years. After payback, with panels warranted for 25 years, the remaining two decades of generation are effectively free electricity, offset only by periodic maintenance and one inverter replacement.

Two factors improve this further. KSEB tariffs have historically trended upward, which increases the value of every unit you displace. And self consumption during daylight hours is worth full retail tariff, so shifting heavy loads such as pumping, washing and ironing to midday improves returns.

Hidden Costs to Ask About Before Signing

  • Net metering application fees and KSEB inspection charges
  • Meter cost, if not included in the quote
  • Any civil work required for structure foundations or cable routing
  • Earthing pit construction, which is often quoted separately
  • Load enhancement charges if your sanctioned load needs revision
  • Additional cost for elevated or customised structures
  • Annual maintenance charges after the free service period expires
  • Whether GST is included in the quoted figure or added on top

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the solar panel installation cost in Kerala for a 3kW system?

A 3kW on grid rooftop solar system in Kerala typically costs between Rs. 1,70,000 and Rs. 2,30,000 before subsidy. After the Rs. 78,000 PM Surya Ghar subsidy, the net cost to the homeowner is roughly Rs. 92,000 to Rs. 1,52,000, depending on component selection and roof conditions.

How much subsidy will I get for a 5kW solar system in Kerala?

Rs. 78,000. The PM Surya Ghar subsidy is capped at 3kW, so a 5kW and a 10kW system receive the same maximum amount. This is why the 3kW size gives the best subsidy to cost ratio.

Is there a separate Kerala state solar subsidy?

No. As things stand, the central PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana is the only active residential rooftop solar subsidy applicable in Kerala. Some states add a top up, but Kerala currently does not.

What size solar system do I need for my home?

Look at your average monthly KSEB consumption. As a rough guide, divide monthly units by 120 to get the approximate kilowatt capacity needed. A home consuming 360 units a month needs around 3kW. A proper assessment also accounts for roof area, shading and how your consumption is distributed across the day.

How long does solar panel installation take?

Physical installation for a residential rooftop system usually takes one to three days after KSEB feasibility and registration. Those utility steps come first (feasibility within 15 days, then registration). Meter commissioning and subsidy credit add further time and are not a fixed countdown from roof work.

Can I pay for solar in instalments or take a loan?

Yes. Several banks and NBFCs offer rooftop solar loans, and the PM Surya Ghar scheme includes provisions for collateral free loans for residential systems up to 3kW at concessional interest rates. Terms vary by lender, so compare before committing.

Does solar increase my property value?

A well installed, documented and warranted rooftop system with active net metering is generally treated as an asset by buyers, since it comes with two decades of reduced electricity cost. Poorly installed systems with no service history can have the opposite effect.

Is GST included in solar installation quotes?

It depends on the vendor. Always ask whether the quoted figure is inclusive of GST. Following the September 2025 GST revision, renewable energy devices attract a concessional 5 percent rate, though composite EPC contracts are taxed on a split basis. Ask for a clear tax breakdown on the quote.

Get an Accurate Price for Your Roof

Ranges are useful for planning, but the only number that matters is the one that reflects your actual roof, your consumption pattern and your location within Kerala. Shading, roof type, distance to the meter board and proximity to the coast can all move the figure meaningfully in either direction.
