ANERT Solar Schemes in Kerala — 2026 Guide

Updated for April 2026. Learn how ANERT fits into Kerala's renewable energy ecosystem, where it intersects with PM Surya Ghar and state-led programs, and what scheme types users should watch.

Last updated: April 13, 2026 Reviewed by: Vishnu M, FGE Solar Sources: ANERT, MNRE
About ANERT

Kerala's State Renewable Energy Agency

Official references used for this page

  • ANERT official site
  • ANERT notice reference PDF
  • MNRE rooftop solar programme page

ANERT (Agency for Non-conventional Energy and Rural Technology) operates under the Kerala Energy Department and implements renewable energy programs across the state. It coordinates central government schemes like PM Surya Ghar, runs state-level programs, and promotes solar adoption through awareness and facilitation.

ANERT's work spans rooftop solar, solar water heating, off-grid rural electrification, solar street lighting, and renewable energy education. For individual homeowners, ANERT's most direct relevance is its role as Kerala's PM Surya Ghar State Implementation Agency and its periodic solar water heater subsidy programs.

ANERT as State Implementation Agency

For PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana, ANERT serves as the State Implementation Agency (SIA) in Kerala. This means all subsidy processing, MNRE empanelment coordination, and scheme monitoring goes through ANERT's framework. FGE Solar, as an MNRE-empanelled installer, works directly within this official process.

Current ANERT Solar Programs in Kerala

Central Scheme — ANERT Coordinated

PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana

ANERT coordinates PM Surya Ghar implementation in Kerala — ensuring MNRE-empanelled installers, subsidy portal processing, and KSEB coordination. This is the primary solar subsidy available to Kerala homeowners, providing up to ₹78,000 for 3kW systems. FGE Solar manages the complete application through the official ANERT-monitored process.

Institutional

Solar Rooftop for Government Buildings

ANERT facilitates solar installation for government offices, schools, hospitals, and panchayat buildings under state and central funding programs. These are typically tendered projects. Educational institutions and local bodies can contact ANERT directly for scheme eligibility.

Local Body

Solar Street Lighting Program

ANERT implements solar street lighting programs for rural panchayats and local bodies — reducing municipal electricity costs while improving village lighting. Panchayats and municipalities can apply through ANERT for funded solar street light installations.

Rural / Tribal

Off-Grid Tribal Electrification

Solar electrification of remote tribal hamlets and forest-fringe settlements not connected to the KSEB grid. Special provisions under state welfare and social development programs. FGE Solar has experience in tribal area solar installations in Wayanad and Idukki.
