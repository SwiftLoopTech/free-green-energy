Available Incentives

Three Ways to Reduce Your Solar Investment in Kerala

₹78,000

PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana

Central government direct subsidy for residential solar. Up to ₹78,000 for 3kW systems. Applied through the national portal with MNRE-empanelled installer.

Eligibility: Residential connection · CFA calculated on the first 3 kW only · official portal and DISCOM checks apply
Full Guide →
Net Metering Credits

KSEB Net Metering

Export surplus solar electricity to KSEB grid. Earn credits against your monthly bill. Worth ₹3,000–₹5,000/month savings for a 3kW system.

Eligibility: Any KSEB consumer with grid-connected solar system · Available statewide
Full Guide →
Various Schemes

ANERT Solar Schemes

Kerala state agency ANERT runs periodic schemes for solar water heaters, off-grid solar, and renewable energy projects. Eligibility varies by scheme and year.

Eligibility: Varies by scheme — BPL households, institutions, rural areas. Contact us for current availability.
