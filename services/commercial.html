Business Case

Why Kerala Businesses Are Investing in Commercial Solar Power

For many businesses, electricity is a recurring operating expense that increases as production, cooling, refrigeration, lighting and equipment use grow.

A correctly sized commercial rooftop solar system generates electricity at the same premises where it is consumed. Businesses with substantial daytime electricity demand can therefore reduce the amount of energy they purchase from the grid during solar-generation hours.

Actual savings depend on your tariff category, energy-consumption profile, solar-system capacity, generation, self-consumption and applicable KSEB/KSERC billing rules.

Reduce Daytime Electricity Costs

Commercial properties often consume a large portion of their electricity during daylight hours. A properly designed solar system can directly supply part of this requirement, reducing grid imports while solar generation is available.

The amount saved varies according to:
  • Electricity tariff
  • Daytime consumption
  • Solar-system capacity
  • Roof conditions
  • Solar generation
  • Metering arrangement
  • Business operating schedule

Improve Long-Term Energy Cost Visibility

A solar system requires upfront capital investment but does not require fuel to generate electricity.

This can help businesses reduce exposure to a portion of future grid-electricity costs, although tariffs and regulatory conditions can change over the life of the system.

Use Available Commercial Rooftop Space

Warehouses, factories, hospitals, schools, hotels and office buildings often have substantial unused roof area.

A commercial rooftop solar project turns suitable roof space into an electricity-generating business asset without requiring additional land.

Tax & Depreciation Considerations

Solar power-generating systems may qualify for depreciation benefits under the applicable income-tax rules.

The tax impact depends on the business entity, ownership model, applicable tax regime, date the asset is placed in service and other conditions.

FGE Solar can provide the project cost and technical documentation required for your financial evaluation, but businesses should confirm depreciation and tax treatment with their chartered accountant or tax adviser.

ESG & Sustainability Objectives

Commercial solar can contribute to an organisation's renewable-energy and emissions-reduction strategy by increasing the share of electricity generated from a renewable source.

Where sustainability reporting is required, generation data from the solar monitoring system can support internal energy reporting.

Lower Grid Dependence During Solar Hours

Solar cannot eliminate every electricity charge or guarantee complete energy independence.

However, it can reduce the amount of electricity imported from the grid during generation hours when matched correctly with the property's load profile.

Who We Serve

Commercial Solar for Every Business Type

FGE Solar designs commercial solar solutions around each property's electricity demand, roof availability and business operations.

Factories & Manufacturing Units

Manufacturing facilities with strong daytime equipment loads can be well suited to commercial solar power. A detailed load profile helps determine how much solar generation can be consumed directly within the facility and what system capacity is technically and financially appropriate.

Warehouses & Logistics Facilities

Warehouses often combine large rooftop areas with lighting, ventilation, office equipment and material-handling loads. A commercial rooftop solar system can make productive use of otherwise unused roof space.

Hospitals & Clinics

Hospitals and healthcare facilities operate significant electrical equipment, HVAC, lighting and support systems. Solar can reduce a portion of daytime grid consumption, while critical backup requirements must continue to be designed separately through the appropriate backup infrastructure.

Schools, Colleges & Educational Institutions

Educational campuses commonly have strong daytime electricity consumption for classrooms, laboratories, offices, cooling and other facilities. Solar can help institutions reduce their long-term electricity operating costs.

Hotels & Resorts

Hotels use electricity for air conditioning, kitchens, water heating, refrigeration, lighting and guest facilities. Commercial solar can offset part of these daytime loads when the available rooftop area and load profile are suitable.

Offices & Commercial Buildings

Corporate offices, business centres and commercial buildings commonly operate during the same hours that rooftop solar is generating electricity. This can create a strong self-consumption profile for solar for commercial buildings.

Supermarkets & Retail Stores

Retail outlets use electricity for refrigeration, cooling, lighting, displays and other equipment throughout the day. A properly sized commercial solar system can reduce part of this recurring grid consumption.

Agricultural & Agro-Processing Businesses

Cold storage, processing, pumping and packaging operations can have substantial electricity requirements. The right solar configuration depends on the consumer category, load pattern, applicable scheme and grid rules.

Religious & Charitable Institutions

Churches, temples, mosques, charitable organisations and community institutions can assess rooftop solar where electricity consumption and suitable roof area justify the investment. Any grant, CSR funding or incentive should be confirmed specifically for the institution before being included in the financial model.

Investment Breakdown

Commercial Solar Panel Cost in Kerala

The commercial solar panel cost cannot be accurately determined from system capacity alone.

Two 100 kW projects can have different investment costs depending on the property, engineering requirements and selected equipment.

Factors affecting commercial solar installation cost include:

System Capacity

Larger systems require more panels, inverter capacity, structure, cabling and associated electrical equipment.

Solar Panel Specification

Panel cost can vary according to:

  • Manufacturer
  • Wattage
  • Module technology
  • Efficiency
  • Product warranty
  • Performance warranty

Commercial Inverter

The inverter configuration depends on:

  • Total array capacity
  • Roof layout
  • Electrical design
  • Grid connection
  • Monitoring requirements
  • Redundancy requirements

Mounting Structure

The cost and design vary between:

  • RCC roofs
  • Metal roofs
  • Industrial sheds
  • Elevated structures
  • Ground-mounted systems

Wind-loading, corrosion protection, waterproofing and structural conditions also influence the design.

Electrical & Safety Infrastructure

Commercial installations can require:

  • AC and DC protection
  • Earthing
  • Lightning protection
  • Isolators
  • Distribution panels
  • Cables
  • Metering infrastructure
  • Transformer-side integration where applicable

Rooftop & Structural Requirements

Additional strengthening, walkway construction, elevated structures or access improvements can increase project cost.

Battery Storage

Battery storage significantly changes the investment.

It should only be included where required by the applicable regulation, operational objective or backup strategy.

Monitoring & O&M Requirements

Larger businesses may require advanced generation monitoring, remote alerts, plant-level analytics and structured maintenance agreements.

Audit Parameters

How to Get an Accurate Commercial Solar Quote

To get a realistic cost of commercial solar energy for your facility, prepare the following details before requesting a quote:

1. Last 3–6 months of KSEB electricity bills
2. Tariff category (LT commercial / industrial or HT consumer)
3. Connected load or contract demand
4. Daytime operating hours and typical working days per week
5. Rooftop type (concrete flat roof, metal sheet shed, tile roof, etc.)
6. Approximate usable roof area
7. Structural condition of the building
8. Any significant shading from trees, parapets or adjacent structures
9. Available location for inverter installation and cable routing
10. Interest in future capacity expansion or battery storage

A credible solar installer will review these parameters and conduct an engineering site survey before providing a firm commercial solar quote.

System Architecture

What Is Included in a Commercial Solar Power System?

A complete commercial solar power system engineered for Kerala conditions includes more than solar panels.

Commercial Solar Panels

Solar PV modules convert sunlight into DC electricity.

Panel selection should consider:

  • Efficiency
  • Module wattage
  • Technology
  • Temperature characteristics
  • Available roof area
  • Manufacturer warranty
  • Performance warranty
  • Service support

Solar Inverters

Inverters convert the DC power generated by the solar panels into AC electricity suitable for use within the facility.

Commercial systems may use one or multiple inverters depending on system capacity, roof layout and electrical design.

Mounting Structure

The mounting structure secures the panels to the roof or ground.

Commercial design must consider:

  • Roof construction
  • Wind load
  • Structural capacity
  • Drainage
  • Access
  • Maintenance
  • Corrosion environment

Electrical Protection

Appropriate AC/DC protection, earthing, isolators and other safety equipment form an essential part of professional commercial solar power installation.

Solar Monitoring System

Monitoring allows facilities teams to track:

  • Energy generation
  • System availability
  • Inverter status
  • Historical output
  • Performance issues

Optional Energy Storage

Battery storage may be included where required by regulation or where the business has a specific operational need.

Battery sizing should be based on the actual objective rather than automatically added to every commercial project.

KSEB Grid Connectivity for Commercial Solar in Kerala

Commercial and industrial grid treatment should not be described as identical.

Under the Kerala State Electricity Regulatory Commission's 2025 Renewable Energy and Related Matters Regulations, eligible industrial consumers may opt for net metering for renewable-energy systems from 1 kW up to 500 kW, subject to the specified conditions.

The regulations also make net billing available to all consumers/prosumers, subject to capacity and other conditions.

The appropriate arrangement for a particular business depends on:
  • Consumer category
  • System capacity
  • Connected load or contract demand
  • Storage requirements
  • Utility feasibility
  • Applicable KSERC rules

For this reason, FGE Solar reviews the customer's electricity connection and proposed system before recommending the grid configuration.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions About Commercial Solar

What are commercial solar power panels?
Commercial solar panels are photovoltaic modules installed to generate electricity for businesses, institutions, factories and other non-residential properties. A complete system typically includes solar panels, inverters, mounting structures, electrical protection, monitoring and grid-integration equipment.
How much does a commercial solar panel system cost in Kerala?
The commercial solar panel cost depends on system capacity, panel and inverter specifications, roof type, mounting structure, electrical work, storage requirements and installation complexity. A site survey and electricity-load analysis are necessary before providing an accurate commercial quotation.
What size commercial solar system does my business need?
The right capacity depends on electricity consumption, daytime demand, connected load or contract demand, roof space, tariff category and applicable utility regulations. FGE Solar reviews recent electricity bills and the operating profile before recommending capacity.
What is the payback period for commercial solar in Kerala?
There is no single payback period for all commercial installations. Payback depends on project cost, electricity tariff, solar generation, self-consumption, applicable depreciation/tax treatment, financing cost, maintenance, and future tariff changes. Businesses with strong daytime electricity consumption may achieve shorter payback than sites where much of the generation would be exported.
Is PM Surya Ghar subsidy available for commercial solar?
PM Surya Ghar Central Financial Assistance is intended primarily for eligible residential household rooftop-solar installations. Commercial and industrial connections should not assume that this residential subsidy applies. Any sector-specific incentive should be verified against the relevant current government scheme before being included in the investment model.
