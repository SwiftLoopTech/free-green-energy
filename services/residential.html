Qualifying Criteria

Is Your Home Ready for Solar?

Solar is a great fit for most Kerala homes. Check the criteria below — if you meet 3 or more, you're almost certainly a strong candidate.

Not sure? Our engineer will assess your property for free and tell you exactly what you qualify for — including your exact subsidy amount.
  • Monthly KSEB bill above ₹2,000

    The higher your bill, the faster your payback. Bills above ₹3,000/month are ideal for rooftop solar.

  • Roof area of at least 150–200 sq ft available

    A 3kW system needs roughly 200 sq ft of shadow-free roof space. More roof = bigger system = bigger savings.

  • South or west-facing roof orientation

    South-facing roofs get maximum sunlight in India. East or west-facing roofs still work well — typically 10–15% less output.

  • KSEB domestic connection (LT-1A/1B/1C)

    PM Surya Ghar subsidy and KSEB net metering are available for domestic electricity connections.

  • Planning to stay in the property for 5+ years

    Solar is a long-term investment. The system significantly increases property value and saves lakhs over its lifetime.
