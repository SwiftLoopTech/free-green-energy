Off Grid Solar System in Kerala for Homes & Remote Properties

Generate, store and use your own solar electricity without depending on a regular grid connection.

It can be suitable for remote homes, farms, plantations, estate properties, houseboats and other locations where grid access is unavailable, expensive or unreliable.

How It Works

How an Off Grid Solar System Works

An off grid solar power system generates electricity during daylight hours, supplies your active loads and stores surplus energy in batteries for use when solar generation is low or unavailable.

Unlike an on-grid system, a standalone off-grid system does not depend on KSEB electricity for normal operation.

1

1. Off Grid Solar Panels

Solar panels convert sunlight into DC electricity.

The panel array must be sized to generate enough energy for both daytime consumption and battery charging.

2

2. MPPT Charge Controller

The charge controller manages electricity flowing from the solar array to the battery bank.

It helps regulate charging voltage and protects the battery system from inappropriate charging conditions.

3

3. Battery Bank

The battery stores solar electricity for:

  • Evening use
  • Nighttime loads
  • Cloudy periods
  • Periods when generation is lower than consumption

Battery capacity is one of the most important factors in a successful off grid solar setup.

4

4. Off Grid Solar Inverter

The inverter converts stored DC electricity into AC electricity that normal household or business appliances can use.

Inverter capacity must be selected according to your peak simultaneous load rather than only the solar-panel capacity.

5

5. Your Electrical Loads

The system supplies the appliances and equipment included in the design.

These may include:

  • Lights
  • Fans
  • Refrigerator
  • Television
  • Wi-Fi router
  • Water pumps
  • Computers
  • Agricultural equipment
  • Selected air-conditioning loads

A properly designed off-grid system starts by deciding exactly which appliances must operate and for how long.

Components

What Is Included in a Complete Off Grid Solar System?

A complete off grid solar system consists of more than panels and batteries.

Off Grid Solar Panels

The off grid solar array generates the electricity required by the property.

Panel selection depends on:

  • Daily energy requirement
  • Available roof or ground area
  • Local shading
  • Module wattage
  • Efficiency
  • Manufacturer warranty
  • Installation conditions

MPPT Charge Controller

The controller manages charging between the solar array and battery bank. Some off-grid inverters include integrated MPPT charging, while other designs use separate charge controllers.

Solar Battery Bank

Battery storage determines how much energy is available after sunset and during periods of poor solar generation.

Common battery options include:

  • Lithium iron phosphate
  • Tubular lead-acid

The correct option depends on budget, usable capacity, maintenance requirements, expected cycling and manufacturer warranty.

Off Grid Solar Inverter

The inverter converts DC battery power into AC electricity. It should be selected according to:

  • Peak appliance load
  • Motor starting current
  • Battery voltage
  • Required output
  • Expansion plans

Mounting Structure

Solar panels may be installed on:

  • RCC roofs
  • Sloping roofs
  • Metal roofs
  • Ground-mounted structures
  • Elevated structures

The mounting design must account for the site and environmental conditions.

Electrical Protection & Earthing

A professional system should include appropriate:

  • DC protection
  • AC protection
  • Isolators
  • Surge protection
  • Earthing
  • Cabling
  • Distribution equipment

Monitoring System

Where supported by the inverter or energy-management system, monitoring can show:

  • Solar generation
  • Battery charge level
  • Electricity consumption
  • Inverter status
  • Fault alerts
Use Cases

Who Needs Off Grid Solar in Kerala?

An off grid power system makes the most sense where a grid connection is unavailable, difficult or insufficient for the property's requirements.

Remote Homes

Properties located far from existing electricity infrastructure can evaluate whether installing a standalone solar system is more practical than extending a grid connection.

Farms & Plantations

Off-grid solar can power selected loads such as:

  • Irrigation pumps
  • Farmhouses
  • Security systems
  • Lighting
  • Small agricultural equipment
  • Monitoring devices

The system must be designed around motor-starting loads and operating hours.

Estate & Hill Properties

Plantation homes, worker accommodation and remote estate buildings in districts such as Idukki and Wayanad may benefit where utility access is limited or unreliable.

Houseboats & Backwater Properties

An appropriately designed standalone solar-and-battery system can reduce reliance on generator operation for selected electrical loads.

The system design must consider limited mounting area, weight and the operating environment.

Construction & Temporary Sites

Off-grid solar can support:

  • Site offices
  • Security lighting
  • CCTV
  • Communications
  • Small tools
  • Temporary accommodation

Remote Commercial Properties

Homestays, eco-resorts and other remote businesses can use off grid solar power where stable grid access is unavailable.

Suitability

Is an Off Grid Solar System Right for Your Home?

An off grid solar system for home is not automatically the best solar option for every property.

It is most suitable when independence from the electricity grid is genuinely required.

Consider Off Grid Solar If

  • Your property has no practical grid connection
  • Extending electricity infrastructure is expensive
  • The existing supply is extremely unreliable
  • You require an independent source of electricity
  • Your electricity loads can be clearly planned and controlled
  • You have enough space for panels and battery equipment

Consider On Grid Solar Instead If

You already have a reliable KSEB connection and your primary objective is reducing electricity costs.

An on-grid system generally avoids the additional cost of a large battery bank.

Consider Hybrid Solar If

You have a grid connection but also need battery backup during power outages.

Hybrid solar can provide a middle path between full off-grid independence and standard on-grid solar.

Sizing

How to Size an Off Grid Solar System

Correct sizing is the most important part of an off grid solar electric system.

If the system is too small, the batteries may run out before the next adequate solar-charging period.

If it is unnecessarily oversized, the initial investment can be much higher than required.

Step 1 – Calculate Daily Electricity Consumption

List every appliance you want the system to operate.

For each appliance:

Power in watts × hours used per day = daily watt-hours

Add all loads to estimate total daily consumption.

Step 2 – Calculate Peak Load

Identify appliances that may operate simultaneously.

The inverter must be capable of supplying this combined load, including motor-starting requirements where applicable.

Step 3 – Decide Required Battery Backup

Determine how long the property should operate without meaningful solar charging.

This may be:

  • Overnight only
  • One day
  • Two days
  • Longer for critical remote applications

Longer autonomy requires substantially more battery capacity.

Step 4 – Size the Solar Array

The solar array must generate enough energy to:

  • Run daytime loads
  • Recharge the battery
  • Compensate for normal system losses

Batteries

Off Grid Solar Battery Options

Battery storage is the key difference between off-grid and standard grid-connected solar.

Lithium Iron Phosphate Batteries

Lithium batteries generally offer:

  • Higher usable capacity
  • Lower maintenance
  • Compact size
  • Faster charging
  • High cycle-life potential

Their upfront cost is usually higher. Actual lifespan depends on battery quality, operating temperature, depth of discharge, cycle frequency and manufacturer specifications.

Tubular Lead-Acid Batteries

Lead-acid batteries generally offer:

  • Lower initial cost
  • Wide availability
  • Familiar service infrastructure

They typically require more maintenance and provide less usable capacity from the same nominal battery rating.

Which Battery Is Better?

There is no single answer for every project.

For long-term permanent installations, lifecycle cost and usable energy should be considered alongside purchase price.

For budget-sensitive or occasional-use applications, lead-acid may still be considered where appropriate.

FGE Solar should specify the battery:

  • Chemistry
  • Nominal capacity
  • Usable capacity
  • Warranty
  • Recommended depth of discharge
  • Expected operating conditions

in the written proposal.

Benefits & Limitations

Benefits and Limitations of Off Grid Solar

Benefits

  • Grid Independence — The property can operate without relying on a normal grid connection.
  • Power After Sunset — Battery storage provides electricity when solar panels are not generating.
  • Suitable for Remote Locations — Off-grid systems can provide electricity where grid extension is impractical.
  • No Generator Fuel for Normal Solar Operation — A properly sized solar-and-battery system can reduce or eliminate routine generator use for the loads it was designed to serve.
  • Predictable System Design — Electricity supply is based on installed generation and storage rather than utility availability.

Limitations

  • Higher Initial Cost — Battery storage makes an off-grid system more expensive than a comparable on-grid installation.
  • Battery Replacement — Battery performance declines over time and should be included in long-term ownership cost.
  • Consumption Must Be Managed — Adding large new appliances may require battery, inverter or panel upgrades.
  • Surplus Electricity Cannot Normally Be Exported — Once batteries are full and loads are satisfied, additional generation may not be usable unless the system has another suitable destination for that energy.
  • Cloudy Weather Matters More — Off-grid systems must be designed conservatively because there is no normal grid supply available to cover an energy deficit.
Subsidy

Does Off Grid Solar Qualify for PM Surya Ghar Subsidy?

The standard PM Surya Ghar Central Financial Assistance for residential consumers is structured around eligible grid-connected rooftop solar installations.

A standalone off-grid solar system that operates without a grid connection does not normally fit that residential CFA route.

If subsidy eligibility is an important objective, compare an on-grid or suitable hybrid configuration and confirm the current scheme requirements before purchase.
