About FGE Solar: Solar Installation Company in Kerala

FGE Solar, also known as Future Green Energy Group, helps homes and businesses make informed, practical decisions about rooftop solar.

Based in Thiruvananthapuram and serving customers across Kerala, we provide residential and commercial solar installation, system design, documentation assistance, KSEB coordination, commissioning and post-installation support.

Our goal is straightforward: make the transition to solar electricity in Kerala easier to understand, properly engineered and financially meaningful for every customer.

Why We Started

Why FGE Solar Was Started

FGE Solar was created in response to a problem experienced by many Kerala households and businesses: electricity consumption was increasing, grid costs were becoming harder to manage and choosing the right solar system remained unnecessarily complicated.

Solar panels had become more accessible, but customers still had important questions:

What size of system does the property actually need?
Is an on-grid, off-grid or hybrid system more suitable?
How much suitable roof area is available?
What will the system realistically generate?
Which components and warranties are included?
Who will handle KSEB applications and metering procedures?
What support will be available after installation?

FGE Solar was established to bring these parts together through one coordinated process.

We do not view solar as a one-day panel installation. We view it as a long-term energy project that begins with understanding the customer’s electricity usage and continues through system design, installation, commissioning, monitoring and after-sales support.

How We Work

The Standards Behind Every FGE Solar Installation

01

Site Assessment Before System Recommendation

Before recommending a system, the FGE Solar team assesses available roof area, orientation, shading, structural conditions, electrical infrastructure and installation access.

We also review the customer’s electricity bills and discuss future requirements such as air conditioning, business expansion or electric-vehicle charging.

02

Design Based on Actual Electricity Usage

The appropriate solar capacity depends on more than roof size.

We consider:

  • Monthly electricity consumption.
  • Daytime and nighttime usage.
  • Available shadow-free area.
  • Property type.
  • Grid availability.
  • Backup requirements.
  • Budget.
  • Future energy needs.
  • Applicable metering and regulatory conditions.
03

Transparent Equipment and Warranty Information

Every proposal should clearly identify:

  • Proposed system capacity.
  • Solar-panel make and model.
  • Inverter make and model.
  • Mounting-structure specification.
  • Electrical protections.
  • Available product warranties.
  • Workmanship coverage.
  • Installation scope.
  • Items not included in the quotation.
04

Installation and Electrical Quality Control

The installation team follows the approved design and checks the mounting structure, panel alignment, cable routing, electrical protection and system connections before commissioning.

Project photographs and commissioning records should be maintained for customer reference and internal quality control.

05

KSEB and Documentation Assistance

For applicable installations, FGE Solar assists customers with the documentation and coordination required for grid connectivity, inspection, metering and eligible government schemes.

Final approvals, metering decisions and subsidy disbursement remain subject to the relevant authorities and applicable regulations.

06

Post-Installation Support

After commissioning, customers receive guidance on:

  • System operation.
  • Generation monitoring.
  • Inverter alerts.
  • Basic maintenance.
  • Panel-cleaning requirements.
  • Warranty procedures.
  • Service-request channels.

Annual maintenance and system inspections are also available based on the selected service plan.

Credentials

Credentials That Support a Compliant Solar Journey

Credentials are important because solar installation involves technical work, electrical connections, utility procedures and, for eligible residential customers, government-scheme documentation.

MNRE Empanelment

FGE Solar is presented as an MNRE-empanelled solar installer.

Where applicable, this supports participation in the official rooftop-solar process. Customer and project eligibility remains subject to the rules of the relevant scheme and authorities.

KSEB Vendor Approval

FGE Solar is presented as a KSEB-approved vendor for eligible solar installations in Kerala.

Our team assists customers with the applicable application, inspection, grid-connectivity and metering procedures.

PM Surya Ghar Portal Registration

FGE Solar is registered on the PM Surya Ghar portal and supports eligible residential customers through the relevant rooftop-solar application and installation process.

Registration does not guarantee that every applicant will qualify or that a subsidy will be released within a fixed timeframe. Final approval and payment remain under the official process.
