We started FGE Solar to solve a simple, persistent problem: Kerala's rising electricity bills. Everything we do is built around solving that problem — completely.

Why We Started

Founded to Change One Number: Your Monthly Bill.

Every year, KSEB tariffs rise. Every year, households pay more for the same electricity. That reality is what FGE Solar was founded to change.

We saw a clear gap: solar technology had become genuinely cost-effective, subsidies made it even more accessible, but most homeowners were still stuck — confused by paperwork, uncertain about companies, and unsure if the ROI was real.

FGE Solar was built to remove every barrier between a homeowner and their solar system. Not just installation — but the entire journey. Subsidy paperwork. KSEB coordination. Net metering applications. Post-install support. The whole thing, handled.

In our first year, we've deployed 700 kW across 50+ installations in Kerala. Every one of those customers has a lower KSEB bill today. That's the number we care about.

Trust & Credentials

Certified. Approved. Accountable.

Three certifications that protect your subsidy, your installation, and your investment.

MNRE Empanelled

Empanelled with the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy, Government of India. This means we are authorized to process PM Surya Ghar subsidies directly on your behalf — you don't have to navigate government portals yourself.

KSEB Approved Vendor

Approved by Kerala State Electricity Board as a qualified solar installer. This approval is essential for net metering applications and for installations under the Soura and E-Kiran programs.

PM Surya Ghar Registered

Registered on the national PM Surya Ghar portal, which means your subsidy application is processed through official channels with full transparency and direct bank transfer to you.
