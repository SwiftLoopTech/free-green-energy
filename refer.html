Program terms

Clear rules, so nobody is surprised

  • Anyone can refer a Kerala property to FGE Solar. You do not need to be an existing customer.
  • The ₹5,000 reward is paid only after the referred customer’s rooftop solar installation with FGE Solar is completed and commissioned.
  • Payment is by UPI or bank transfer to the referrer named on the form.
  • There is no limit on the number of successful referrals.
  • Self-referrals and properties already in active discussion with FGE Solar are not eligible.
  • The referred person receives a free site visit and a normal FGE quote. They do not receive a separate cash reward.
  • FGE Solar contacts the referred person directly. Please refer only people who would reasonably expect a call about solar.
  • This offer is current as of September 2026 and may be updated. The terms published on this page apply to referrals submitted while they are live.
