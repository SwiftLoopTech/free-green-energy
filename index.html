The Problem

Your KSEB Bill Is Going to Keep Rising.

For monthly domestic consumption up to 250 units, KSEB uses telescopic energy-charge slabs. Higher consumption moves more units into higher-priced slabs; above 250 units, separate non-telescopic rates apply.

Solar can reduce billed grid energy, but the result depends on consumption, generation, current net-metering accounting, and fixed or other approved charges.

Eligible households can receive central CFA up to ₹78,000.

Government Subsidy
₹78K

PM Surya Ghar supports eligible residential rooftop solar.

The central CFA is ₹30,000/kW for the first 2 kW and ₹18,000 for the third kW, capped at ₹78,000. Payment follows the official portal process and DISCOM inspection/approval.

  • No private documents - Aadhaar, KSEB bill, and bank details
  • FGE registers your application through the PM Surya Ghar portal
  • Net metering approval, installation, inspection, and commissioning
  • CFA is transferred to the approved beneficiary bank or loan account; no installer can guarantee a payment date
