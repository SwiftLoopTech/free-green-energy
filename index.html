Reduce Rising KSEB Bills with Solar Electricity in Kerala

Electricity consumption in Kerala is increasing as homes and businesses use more air conditioners, appliances, equipment and electric vehicles. Higher grid usage can result in substantially larger electricity bills, particularly for high-consumption properties.

A correctly sized rooftop solar system generates electricity during daylight hours and reduces the amount of power you need to purchase from the grid. The actual savings depend on your consumption pattern, roof conditions, system capacity, electricity tariff, metering arrangement and applicable charges.

For eligible properties, solar electricity in Kerala can provide:

  • Lower dependence on grid electricity
  • Greater control over long-term energy expenses
  • Productive use of unused rooftop space
  • Cleaner electricity for homes and businesses
  • Potential subsidy support for eligible residential systems
  • Additional resilience when combined with battery storage

Government Subsidy

Get PM Surya Ghar Subsidy Support in Kerala

Residential households in Kerala can receive Central Financial Assistance (CFA) under the PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana when installing a grid-connected rooftop solar system.

The current central subsidy structure provides:

  • ₹30,000 per kW for capacity up to 2 kW
  • ₹18,000 for the additional third kW
  • Maximum central subsidy of ₹78,000 for systems of 3 kW or more

This subsidy is available only to residential consumers and is processed through the official government portal after application, installation and inspection.

FGE Solar can assist with:

  • Application guidance through the PM Surya Ghar portal
  • Preparation of necessary documentation
  • System proposal and technical file submission
  • Installation after approval
  • DISCOM inspection and commissioning
  • Subsidy status tracking through the official process

Final subsidy eligibility, approval timelines and fund release are determined only by the competent authority and electricity distribution company. No installer can guarantee final eligibility or the date of fund release.
