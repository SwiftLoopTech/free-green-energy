The Problem

Your KSEB Bill Is Going to Keep Rising.

Kerala uses a telescopic tariff system - the more you consume, the more you pay per unit. High-usage homes pay the most expensive slab rates. And those rates go up almost every year.

Solar doesn't just reduce your bill. It eliminates the most expensive units you consume first - cutting your bill by 80-90% in most cases.

And right now, the government pays ₹78,000 of the cost for you.

High bills are fixable. Kerala homes with usable roof space can turn rising KSEB tariffs into long-term savings.

Government Subsidy - Available Now, Not Forever

₹78,000

Under PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana, the Government of India pays ₹78,000 directly into your bank account for a 3 kW system.

This subsidy is first-come-first-served. Once capacity is filled in your area, it's gone.
