Program terms

Clear rules, so nobody is surprised

  • This is FGE Solar’s channel partner program. It is separate from the consumer Refer & Earn ₹5,000 offer.
  • Registration is free. Lead submission opens only after FGE Solar approves the partner account.
  • Approved partners submit customer enquiries through the partner portal at erp.fgesolar.in/partner. Each lead is tagged to the partner who submitted it.
  • FGE Solar handles site visit, quotation, KSEB process, installation, and applicable subsidy assistance. Partners are not required to attend meetings unless they choose to.
  • Commission is recorded against successful partner jobs and shown in the portal as pending, approved, or paid. The amount is confirmed during onboarding. Site visits and quotes alone do not trigger payout.
  • Duplicate enquiries, self-leads, and properties already in active discussion with FGE Solar may not qualify.
  • Please introduce only people who would reasonably expect a call about rooftop solar.
  • These terms are current as of September 2026 and may be updated. The version published on this page applies to applications submitted while it is live.
